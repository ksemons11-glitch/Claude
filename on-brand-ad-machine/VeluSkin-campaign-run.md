### The 10 winning angles in the category (what's working right now)
1. **Injectables alternative** — "like I've had injectables", "prefer patches over injectables", needle-free.
2. **Expert / surgeon authority** — a doctor or plastic surgeon endorses the overnight fix.
3. **Overnight / instant speed** — "results in 1–2 hours", "one night", "5 seconds to apply".
4. **Cost-per-use value** — "~$1.50 a use", reusable up to 20×.
5. **Social proof at scale** — "200,000+ users", "87% prefer…", testimonial quotes.
6. **Retail / "as seen on"** — Ulta, Shark Tank, Amazon #1.
7. **Emotional problem-story** — relatable narrative about frown lines "talking".
8. **Whole-face coverage** — "a patch for every line" (forehead → under-eye → chest).
9. **Under-eye / dark-circles specific** — puffiness, tired eyes, glass-skin glow.
10. **Reusable / "last patches you'll buy"** — sustainability + economy.
